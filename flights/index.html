How it works

1 · Type the flight number

Use the number on your ticket, like EK600 or PK 301. We turn it into the radio callsign planes broadcast (EK600 → UAE600).

2 · See it live

The flight's position, height and speed update every 20 seconds on the map, with a trail of where it has been.

3 · Not flying yet?

Planes only show while their transponder is on, roughly from push-back to landing. For gates, delays and schedules use the FlightAware link.
